ByteTuck support

ByteTuck privacy and data-deletion requests

ByteTuck is provided by Franko Taljaard Apps. It has no ByteTuck account and processes images on your Android device. You can ask us to delete support emails and attachments you have sent to us.

Request deletion of support correspondence

  1. Email vmadmsup@gmail.com with the subject “ByteTuck data deletion request”.
  2. Use the same email address you used to contact support, and identify the correspondence you want deleted, or ask us to delete all of your ByteTuck support correspondence.
  3. We will reply if we need to verify the request and confirm when it is completed. Do not send a password, payment-card details or copies of your images to make this request.

What is deleted or retained

For a verified request, we delete the requested support emails and attachments from the developer's active mailbox and trash, including our copies of the related correspondence. Once the request is completed, we do not keep a separate developer-controlled copy for an additional retention period.

Our email provider may retain service records or backups under its own policies. We cannot delete records independently held by Google, including advertising, consent, Google Play purchase or payment records, or records held by other apps and storage providers you choose.

Images and app data on your phone

The developer does not receive the images you process in ByteTuck. Use Android Settings → Apps → ByteTuck → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall ByteTuck, to remove its local app data. This resets app settings and temporary files. It does not delete originals or copies you saved or shared elsewhere; remove those through their storage app or recipient.

Deleting local app data does not cancel your Google Play purchase. Restore purchase checks the purchase available to your current Google Play account.

Google services and privacy choices

Open ByteTuck → Settings → Privacy choices when available to change the choices offered by Google's consent service. You can also review Google's ad controls, Google Account data and privacy controls and Google Play Help. These are separate from a request to delete our support correspondence.
