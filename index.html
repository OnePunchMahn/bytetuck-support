ByteTuck

Make it fit.

Choose an image, set the maximum file size, then save or share a smaller copy. ByteTuck processes images on your Android device and preserves your original.

Use presets from 100 KB to 5 MB or enter a custom limit. Check the output preview and measured size before sharing. Compression may change quality and dimensions.

The Android app is being prepared for release. This page is its support website; it does not offer image uploads or downloads of an unfinished app.
